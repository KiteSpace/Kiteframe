(POST–PROJECT PANEL REFACTOR)

GOAL CONTEXT (FOR REPLIT)
We are moving Layers toward:
• Workflow-first grouping (not node-first)
• No edges displayed
• A workflow exists ONLY if it has ≥2 edges
• Standalone nodes remain ungrouped
• Decision-tree workflows must be readable (not just linear lists)
• Layers must persist per project and stay in sync with canvas
