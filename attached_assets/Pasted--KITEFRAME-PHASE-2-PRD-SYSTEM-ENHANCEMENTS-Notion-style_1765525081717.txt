================================================================
SECTION 2 — DOCUMENT OUTLINE / TOC
================================================================

GOAL:
Provide Notion-like navigation for long PRDs.

---
2.1 Outline Generation
---

Input:
PRD sections array

Generate outline entries from:
- Section titles
- Section IDs

DO NOT infer hierarchy from content.
DO NOT add nesting logic yet.

---
2.2 UI Placement
---

Outline appears:
- Inline at top of PRD
- OR as a narrow left column inside the Project tab

MUST:
- Be text-only
- No cards
- No icons beyond chevron or bullet
- Scroll-sync with content

---
2.3 Scroll Sync
---

Implementation:
- Each DocSection has a stable DOM id
- Clicking outline item scrolls to section
- IntersectionObserver highlights active section

DO NOT use heavy libraries.

================================================================
SECTION 3 — INLINE AI SUGGESTIONS
================================================================

GOAL:
AI assists writing without overwriting user content.

---
3.1 Trigger
---

Per DocSection:
- Add “Suggest improvements” button
- Button invokes AI for THAT SECTION ONLY

---
3.2 AI Output Rules
---

AI returns:
- Suggested replacement text
- Optional explanation

AI MUST NOT:
- Modify content automatically
- Write to storage directly

---
3.3 UX
---

Display suggestions as:
- Diff-style preview OR
- Ghost text block below section

User actions:
- Accept → replaces section text
- Reject → dismiss suggestion

---
3.4 Persistence
---

Accepted AI edits:
- Update PRD version number
- Preserve manualEditedAt timestamps

Rejected suggestions:
- Not stored
